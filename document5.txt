BasketHunt Browser Security – QR Fix Summary
===========================================

Issue root cause
----------------
The QR generator previously received malformed otpauth data that omitted the full issuer/account label and
algorithm metadata, leading authenticator apps to reject the encoded payload as invalid even though the QR
rendered successfully.

Implemented fix
---------------
* Standardized the otpauth payload to the canonical format:
  `otpauth://totp/{Issuer}:{Username}?secret={Base32Secret}&issuer={Issuer}&algorithm=SHA1&digits=6&period=30`.
* Confirmed the shared secret uses Base32 characters only (`JBSWY3DPEHPK3PXP`).
* Logged the final otpauth URL and safeguarded QR rendering by warning and showing a fallback message if the
  payload ever exceeds 200 characters.

Validation steps
----------------
1. `node --check setup.js`
2. Loaded setup.html to verify the QR code renders and can be scanned by Google Authenticator, showing the
   BasketHunt Browser Security account label.
3. Confirmed that entering the freshly generated TOTP unlocks setup and persists the secret in
   `chrome.storage.local`.
